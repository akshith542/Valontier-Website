Our Story

It started with one corner and a good cup of coffee.

In 2019, two neighbors took over a quiet storefront on the corner of Maple and Main with a simple idea: build the kind of café they always wished their block had.

What began as a single espresso machine and a tray of morning pastries has grown into a gathering place — the spot where regulars are greeted by name, students settle in for the afternoon, and first dates turn into anniversaries.

We still bake everything in-house, still pull every shot by hand, and still believe the best businesses are the ones that feel like they belong to the neighborhood. Because this one does — to all of you.
